I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at TU Wien, working on the [Unaxima project](https://www.unaxima.com/) in collaboration with [Juan Aguilera](https://juan.ag/).

I completed my [PhD](https://lehouerou.fr/files/these.pdf) under the supervision of [Ludovic Patey](https://ludovicpatey.com) and [Julien Cervelle](https://jc.lacl.fr) focusing on the reverse mathematical aspects of several theorems, primarily coming from Ramsey theory. 

You can download my [CV](https://lehouerou.fr/files/CV.pdf) or my [research statement](https://lehouerou.fr/files/Research_statement.pdf)
